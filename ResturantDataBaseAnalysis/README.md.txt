# 🍽️ Restaurant Sales Analysis – MySQL Project

This project showcases an exploratory analysis of a restaurant's menu and order data using MySQL. Through a series of structured SQL queries, I extracted valuable insights about sales trends, menu pricing, and customer ordering behavior.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **MySQL**
- SQL Workbench / Command Line
- Relational database structure (tables: `menu_items`, `orders`, `order_details`, etc.)

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The goal of this project was to analyze a restaurant’s sales and menu performance using SQL by answering a set of specific business questions.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Questions Answered

1. 🏷️ **What are the least and most expensive items on the menu?**  
2. 🍝 **How many Italian dishes are on the menu?**  
3. 💲 **What are the least and most expensive Italian dishes on the menu?**  
4. 📊 **How many dishes are in each category?**  
5. 📈 **What is the average dish price within each category?**  
6. 📅 **What is the date range of the table?**  
7. 🧾 **How many orders were made within this date range?**  
8. 🍽️ **How many items were ordered within this date range?**  
9. 🔢 **Which orders had the most number of items?**  
10. 🧮 **How many orders had more than 12 items?**  
11. 🥇 **What were the least and most ordered items? What categories were they in?**  
12. 💰 **What were the top 5 orders that spent the most money?**  
13. 🔎 **View the details of the highest spend order. Which specific items were purchased?**
